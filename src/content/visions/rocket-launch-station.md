## the vision

[Write your picture of it here, in your own words.] A compact launch complex for small rockets carrying cubesats and small satellites, run as a joint project between the state, universities and private launch companies. Sri Lanka stops buying space access and starts selling it.

## why here

- About 6°N of the equator: the Earth's spin gives eastward launches a free push.
- Open sea to the south and east, so rocket stages fall into water, not villages.
- A deep-water port and an international airport already sit nearby for heavy cargo.
- Dry-zone weather on the south-east coast: [check rainfall and cloud data].

## pros

- New high-skill jobs for engineers who now leave the country.
- Foreign income from launch contracts.
- Gives students a real reason to study physics and engineering.
- Puts Sri Lanka on the map of space nations.

## cons

- Very high upfront cost: [YOUR ESTIMATE].
- Possible impact on Bundala and Yala wildlife areas nearby.
- Needs treaties, export rules and launch insurance.
- Small market, strong competition from neighbours.
